# Sign in and find your way

**Purpose.** Before anyone connects a repository or reads a number, they have to learn what this
product is, get in, find out where the deployment has got to, and be able to look up any word they
meet. The journey is the shell: the one sentence a sponsor reads before they have a role
("Measures what an AI builder can be trusted to change in your repository, graded by your own
tests."), a session with a role chip, "This is where the deployment is on the way from an empty
install to a change delivered under evidence", the glossary and the eight bundled guides, and a
designed way back from an address that does not exist.

**Entry → exit.** Entry is a link to the deployment, a bookmarked screen that bounces to
`/login?next=`, or an unknown address that bounces the same way. Exit is a person who holds a
session, knows their role and why a button is missing, can say which step of the journey they are
on (`Journey · start` on Home, `Journey · n of 4 · Step` on the steps), has the next task named for
them by Continue, and can reach the definition of any term and the section of any guide in one
click from every screen. Nothing on this journey spends money or writes anything but a session.

**Non-goals.** No repository is connected, nothing is measured, no cell is signed and no account is
created, reset or re-roled here: those are `dod.journey.connect-a-repository`,
`dod.journey.measure`, `dod.journey.sign-off-a-cell` and `dod.journey.deploy-and-go-live`. The
journey does not teach the method — the guides do — and it does not recover an account, which is an
admin's or the host operator's act (`dod.journey.recover-an-account`).
