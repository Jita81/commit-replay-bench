# Sign off a cell

**Purpose.** An approver — a second person, never the operator who queued the runs — records that they reviewed a cell's evidence and read one accepted change, so the cell reads human-verified and the factory may open pull requests for it (ONBOARDING step 7; eyebrow "Journey · 3 of 4 · Decisions · sign-off").

**Entry → exit.** Starts when a cell routes `deliver` and has no active sign-off: it appears on Decisions as "clears the bar — attest it or decline" with an Attest button (an admin has first added the approver's account in Settings, Home task 7). Ends with a hash-chained `crb.signoff.v3` record that names the approver, the kind of account, the policy and its thresholds, the evidence snapshot and the attested row; the cell's verification tier is lifted on the map, its route unchanged, and Home task 6 reads Completed while the record is not stale.

**Non-goals.** The journey does not change a route, a point or an interval. It does not sign a cell whose evidence the approver produced (`same_actor`, no setting relaxes it), a cell with a false-Q1 row, or one whose oracle was never measured. It does not delete a sign-off: revocation is a newer row with a reason. It does not record the human review of a diff (the Review panel on `/runs/:id` does) and it does not decide "decline": an unsigned cell keeps its route.
