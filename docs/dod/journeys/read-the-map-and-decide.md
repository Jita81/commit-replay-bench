# Read the map and decide

**Purpose.** Know, for one repository and per (class × size) cell, whether the instrument is
trustworthy here, what the builder may be trusted to do (deliver, calibrate, granularize or
human, with the reason code) and what is waiting on a person — and leave with the act for
your role. Everyone reads; the operator decides between more attempts and stronger tests; the
approver sees what waits for a signature. (ONBOARDING Step 5 "Read the map (everyone)" and
Step 6 "Before anyone signs anything".)

**Entry → exit.** Arrives from Home's "Read the baseline" task or Continue, or the nav step
"Baseline", after a measurement has put rows on the map. Leaves with: the three gate readings
and whether the numbers are provisional; every cell's route with its n, 95% Wilson interval
and apparatus; the licence sentence for the signed cell (what may be said and what may not);
one cell's full evidence and its route decision with the reason code and the policy in force;
and the decisions inbox with the act for your role — Attest (`/signoff`), Sign a gap or Decide
(`/factory`), Read why (`/routing`), Investigate (`/ledger`) — or "<role> acts" when it is not
yours.

**Non-goals.** No act is recorded on these four pages; each act is a link to the page that
records it (sign-off, factory, ledger). Nothing is run from here except the operator's link to
the run form from an empty map. No repository-wide rate, no "the AI can do X%", no throughput
headline. The ledger (`/ledger`) belongs to the operate journey and is reached by its door.
