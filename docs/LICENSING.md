# Licensing

**Commit Replay Bench is open source under the Apache License 2.0** (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`;
DECISION-LOG DL-028, 2026-09-15). The operator's decision reversed the earlier
source-available choice (DL-015/DL-025): the product is to be shared with delivery teams
who will improve it, and the instrument that graded a client's evidence must be readable,
re-runnable and modifiable by the client without a commercial agreement.

## Why Apache-2.0 rather than MIT

- An express **patent grant** from every contributor (§3), with the defensive termination
  clause — what a public-sector buyer's legal review expects to see.
- A **NOTICE** mechanism (§4(d)) that carries attribution and the AI-authorship statement
  through downstream distributions.
- GPL-3.0-compatible; OSI-approved; the licence most UK-government and NHS open-source
  guidance points at by default.

## What a reviewer will find in the repository

- `LICENSE` — the canonical Apache-2.0 text; `NOTICE` — copyright, the v1 MIT history and
  the AI-authorship statement.
- `pyproject.toml` — SPDX `Apache-2.0` and the `License :: OSI Approved :: Apache Software
  License` classifier; the container image is labelled `org.opencontainers.image.licenses=Apache-2.0`
  (`.github/workflows/release.yml`).
- Third-party inventories: the Python SBOM (CycloneDX, CI `sbom` job) — no GPL/AGPL, one
  LGPL, four MPL; the npm tree of `ui/` is to be inventoried before the first tag.
- The IP-ownership statement: substantial parts of the code were generated by AI systems
  under human direction (`docs/reviews/2026-09-13-critical-friend.md` §6). Copyright in
  computer-generated works (CDPA 1988 s.9(3)) and the model provider's output terms are
  part of the legal pack the owner holds.
- Contributions: a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off (or CLA) is required before
  an external commit is accepted — `docs/CONTRIBUTING.md`.
